Parser moves from3ASCIIletters to bounded catalogknowncode/longestalias scanner,exactamountgrammar; symbols/Chinesealiases and explicitcodepairconsume boundary, wholeinputreject. Numeric-leadingcodes matched longest first before number only when followed by a new moneyamount. Finiteinput4fraction/max1e12existinglimits; conversion2decimalreferenceonly remains. Userprefix/suffix/mixedshortlist preserved.
UI preserves original Form/Field and uses exact released SelectState<SearchableVec<CurrencyChoice>>::searchable(true), Select::search_placeholder/menu_width/menu_max_h. Plain Vec does not filter; official SearchableVec owns case-insensitive title matching. No Combobox/new controller or component-source change. CurrencyChoice includes code+API name+common Chinese name/USD aliases. Catalog revision changes defer set_items and committed selection restoration; status-only notifications do not clear search. Existing unavailable target remains labelled, not silently reset.

## Limitations

ALZ method3, encrypted split/DLZ combinations and unsampled non-ASCII-password/descriptor-time producer profiles are not declared supported. EGG other solid methods, producer split-solid/encrypted-solid, split/encrypted/solid AZO, encrypted Stored/LZMA or LEA+Bzip2 producer profiles and unsampled non-ASCII passwords remain separately unqualified. Locale/parent-relative names return UnsupportedMethod; encrypted filenames/Global Encrypt and short unauthenticated profiles return UnsupportedEncryption. Declaration-refusal fixtures do not qualify encrypted payload support. No POSIX uid/gid/permission restoration, ALZ/EGG creation or editing is added.

Physical UIA/HWND/IME/DPI/user visual approval and installed/Release acceptance remain separate. Independent acceptance covers only the declared capability rows and recorded source/worker/native/TestPlatform levels. B1–B6 acceptance records are unchanged.

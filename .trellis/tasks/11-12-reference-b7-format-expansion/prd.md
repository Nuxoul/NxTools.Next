Implement REF-19 followed by REF-20 under **Phase E — Format expansion and ZIP editing**. User explicitly approved starting B7. B1–B6 remain accepted at their recorded boundaries; B6-01 is closed. B7 does not close the parent phase or accept the complete twenty-item programme.

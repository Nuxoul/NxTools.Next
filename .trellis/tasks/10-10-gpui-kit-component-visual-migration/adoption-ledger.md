## Evidence rules
- No ordinary Switch/Radio/Input/Button is exempt solely because its parent is a custom business list.
- `appearance(false)` is allowed only when an actual released compound component owns the single outer frame, or a named role/capability exception is verified. It is not globally forbidden inside Kit's own implementation.
- The official compact tier may change a control's height; compare actual rendered controls against Kit reference under the same theme. Outer HWND budgets and feature reachability stay fixed.
- Do not mark a row adopted from an import or renamed namespace. Record the production constructor and removal of conflicting old chrome.
- Foundation requires an actually inspected production shell/settings pilot and user approval before remaining stages. Whole-app visual approval remains separate from automatic tests and isolated96DPI message-policy checks.

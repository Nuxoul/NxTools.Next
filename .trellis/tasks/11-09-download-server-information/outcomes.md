## Known issues and acceptance limits

- Current `message_contract.rs::every_command_carries_a_correlation_id_origin_and_version` expects8 while the existing product constant is9:14passed/1failed. This slice does not change the protocol version or that assertion.
- Strict Desktop Clippy remains blocked by three `settings_view.rs` private-interface diagnostics and `browser_handoff_impl.rs:436` nonminimal_bool. No blanket suppression or unrelated repair.
- Full Desktop suite is not certified green. Its prior run retained missing archive-native exports plus independent create/menu failures; scoped results do not replace that result.
- This is source/owned-fixture delivery. No package/install/deployed replacement, service/registry mutation, reference-project execution, browser-extension installation, commit or push. Physical pointer/IME/DPI and human visual acceptance remain separate.
- Response time is HTTP probe time, not Mister's handshake-derived approximate RTT, ICMP Ping or current connection-quality score. Country/CDN lookup is not included. Missing historical observations remain unknown until an original fresh probe occurs.
- Research identified existing Firefox Cookie-store propagation and link-panel setting/routing gaps. These are recorded in the reference note, not changed by this slice. No comparative Mister/Ghost performance benchmark was executed.

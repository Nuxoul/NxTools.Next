## Limits and remaining acceptance

- Not enabled for Deflate, encryption,ZIP64/SFX,7z/TAR/RAR/streams,split sets,other filesystems/different volumes or initially sparse/compressed/encrypted/reparse/multiply-linked sources. Initial4096regular-file/32MiBjournal/64KiBrecord/44KiBretained-structure bounds remain the accepted matrix, not implicit all-archive support.
- Only complete-file continuation and recorded-ready publication are supported. An interrupted incomplete partial without ready preserves source/output/authority and refuses; missing or externally changed indispensable output cannot be recovered from already consumed payload. No promise to restore the full original archive or rebuild a standard remaining archive.
-61actual process tests do not establish hardware/VM power-loss durability. Native112/1117/5 are injected at the real owner, not physical disk-full/device-failure experiments.
- GPUI simulated input/render and nativeCOM/Windows fixtures are not physical Desktop interaction, visual/user or mixed-DPI acceptance. Release fault gate is tested; formal package/release/install is not.

No concrete defect or remaining blocker was found for this bounded implementation. Do not describe this scoped acceptance as all formats/power-loss/user/release completion. Broader support remains a separate qualification, not a reason to redo the accepted first six steps.
